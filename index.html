🎯 Exercise Guide & Pose Detection

🤚 Elbow to Shoulder - What AI Checks:

💪
  • ✅ Elbow is bent (up to 100 degrees)
  • ✅ Elbow is raised towards the shoulder
  • ✅ Closer the elbow to the shoulder, higher the score!

💡 Bend your elbow and bring it up close to your shoulder. You can do one arm or both!

🦶 Seated Leg Stretch - What AI Checks:

🦵
  • ✅ Sit upright with shoulders above hips
  • ✅ Extend one or both knees to straighten your leg
  • ✅ Leg forms a straight line from hip to ankle
  • ✅ Raise the leg slightly for maximum points

💡 Sit down and straighten out one or both of your legs! Raise them slightly for bonus points.

🎮 How to Score Points:

• 76-99% accuracy: +1 point (hold briefly)

• 100% accuracy: +2 points (hold for 2-3 seconds)

• Build your streak for confetti celebration!
